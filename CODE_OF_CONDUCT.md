# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful experience for
everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression,
level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include respectful
technical discussion, accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best
for the project and its users. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal
attacks, publishing private information, and other conduct that would reasonably be
considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to a repository maintainer. Reports will be
reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove
comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the
community.
